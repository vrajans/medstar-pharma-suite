# Roadmap

| Release | Window | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **MVP (Pilot)** | Months 0–4 | Single/multi-store setup, product & batch master, POS (online + offline), H/H1/X/NDPS compliance, purchase & GRN & returns, inventory & expiry & recall, auto-accounting & core reports, GSTR-1/3B/CMP-08 data, storefront + Rx upload + pharmacist verification + delivery + payments + WhatsApp |
| **R2** | Months 4–8 | Chain HQ, stock transfer + e-way bill, B2B e-invoice, NPPA ceiling check, bank & gateway reconciliation, ITC vs GSTR-2B, physical audit app, refill subscriptions, loyalty, substitutes & sister-store fulfilment, Hindi UI, Tauri print bridge, inspection pack, habit-forming alerts |
| **R3** | Months 8–12 | ABDM/ABHA & e-prescriptions, ONDC seller, distributor EDI, IoT cold-chain, CDSCO NSQ feed, AI demand forecasting, teleconsult partner, mobile native apps |

## Not now (explicit)
Own delivery fleet tracking maps · insurance claims · clinic EMR · wholesale distribution module · doctor incentive tracking.
