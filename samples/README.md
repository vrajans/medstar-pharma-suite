# Sample data for testing

- `products-sample.csv` — 15 products across OTC, H, H1 (habit-forming), cold chain, a Nil-rated drug (Imatinib, `gst_override` 0) and an 18 % wellness item. Import at **Products → Import CSV**.
- `opening-stock-sample.csv` — batches for those products, including one batch expiring next month (Dolo DL2511), one already expired (Pan 40 PN4410 — imported with a warning and blocked at the counter) and loose tablets. Load at **Inventory → Load opening stock** after the products.

Barcodes, batches and prices are illustrative, not real product data.
